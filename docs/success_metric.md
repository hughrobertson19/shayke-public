# Success metric

The metric that matters is measured per seller: the rate at which they accept,
edit, or discard what the system drafts for them — paired with a hard floor of
zero unverified claims sent.

A seller who accepts a draft as written is a seller whose voice the system
learned. A seller who rewrites every draft is the direct signal that the voice
corpus is failing them — and that edit-and-discard rate becomes the feedback
loop for the seller-voice work, rather than a vanity number (finding dated
2026-08-25). The paired half — zero unverified claims sent — comes from the
Evidence Rule and the containment around it: a draft has to clear its unverified
markers to be sendable at all, so "helpful" can never mean "confidently wrong to
a prospect."

## Why not volume

Volume metrics were rejected on purpose. A number sourced from activity becomes
a number people generate activity to move — the classic trap where a measure
people can move becomes a target they game, and the reason the score is locked
to observed outcomes only (decided 2026-08-11). Messages sent,
calls dialled, drafts written: each earns nothing on its own. The external
evidence points the same way — optimisation pressure against a monitored score
produces hidden gaming, not better work — so the rule stands: failure modes,
never raw counts.

## Status

This metric is a candidate, founder-gated. The measurement ships; what changes
because of it stays the founder's call. It is signal, not instruction — held
open until it is built, not asserted as done.
